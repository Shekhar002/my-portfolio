## Challenge

Shipment planning relied on logistics data that was incomplete and inconsistent.

## Transformation

- Reconciled **500+ shipment records** in Excel with XLOOKUP, raising accuracy and completeness to **98%**.
- Built a **React prototype** for shipment planning.

## Result

In testing, the prototype reduced planning effort by an estimated **35%**. The cost-benefit analysis and solution gap assessment went to the **Executive Leadership Team** to evaluate wider adoption.
